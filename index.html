WHO WE ARE & WHAT WE DO

Our Mission, Vision & Values

The principles that inspire our educational journey.

01

Our Mission

Smart International School always seeks to educate and develop the whole student: mind, body, and spirit, to assume a position of leadership and understanding of technology and its impact on the global community.

With creative co-curricular activities and a character-building program alongside academic learning and teaching strategies, we nurture our students' development to become more confident, caring, and responsible individuals who can cooperate in a diverse and wider community.

02

Our Vision

  • Providing a positive school atmosphere where every student reaches their full potential and achieves academic excellence through intellectual responsibility and curiosity.
  • Achieving intellectual, physical, and emotional well-being through an innovative program.
  • Promoting teamwork to discover and build global cross-cultural competence that prepares students for an advanced global society.
03

School Values

  • Enhancing students' personal identity and character.
  • Preparing students to live and work in a globally advanced atmosphere.
  • Creating a learning environment that supports students' emotional, social, and academic growth.
  • Encouraging students to help and serve the community.
  • Tolerance and acceptance for all.
